# Apply to become a reviewer

Researchers with relevant expertise can apply through the
[reviewer application form](https://ai4sbench.org/reviewers/).

1. Provide your name, contact email, research areas, affiliation, and a short
   account of your experience. A GitHub account can be supplied with the
   application; it is required to publish reviews later.
2. Submit the application and wait for the project team to review it. The
   application itself does not grant reviewer access.
3. After approval, sign in to [AI4S-Bench](https://ai4sbench.org) with the
   approved GitHub identity and open a proposal from the
   [task board](https://ai4sbench.org/tasks/).

The project team handles the approval and follow-up through the contact
details in your application. Once access is enabled, use the
[reviewer guide](review-proposal.md) to publish a review.
